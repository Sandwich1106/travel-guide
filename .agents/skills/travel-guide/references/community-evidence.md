# Cross-platform reviews / 多平台评价

## Sources / 来源选择

| Decision / 决策 | Sources to try where relevant and readable / 按目的地与可读性选择 |
| --- | --- |
| Neighborhoods, itinerary trade-offs, visitor pitfalls / 住宿片区、路线取舍、避雷 | [Reddit r/travel](https://www.reddit.com/r/travel/) and destination subreddits, [Tripadvisor Forums](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ForumHome), [Rick Steves Travel Forum](https://www.ricksteves.com/travel-forum) for Europe, [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/) for orientation |
| Exact venue, photos, recent reports / 具体地点、照片、近期实测 | [Google Maps reviews](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6230175), Tripadvisor listings, and destination-specific sources such as [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/) for Japan dining or [NAVER Map](https://map.naver.com/) for South Korea |
| Hotels and transport / 住宿与交通 | [Booking.com guest reviews](https://www.booking.com/reviews.html), accessible hotel booking platforms, airport or rail forums and independent trip reports |
| Accessible alternatives / 可访问替代 | Local-language guides and independent blogs, operator pages, local maps, [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) for locations, Chinese-language firsthand reports on 携程、马蜂窝 or 小红书 where readable |

## English workflow

1. Shortlist first. For each recommendation that materially affects cost, time, or comfort, match the branch/address, hotel and room category when relevant, attraction entrance or ticket type, and recent visit period. Search the exact local name and targeted terms such as “noise”, “stairs”, “queue”, “tourist trap”, or the local-language equivalents. Check praise and criticism.
2. Aim for two or more independent source types or platforms per consequential pick when evidence is available. Look beyond star averages. Prioritize specific firsthand reports with visit dates, photos, or relevant traveler profiles. Compare equivalent seasons, time slots, rooms, and budgets. Treat copied, syndicated, or sponsored accounts accordingly and count repeated text once.
3. Give a compact comparison for each key pick: **best for / recurring strengths / recurring drawbacks / where reports disagree / what I'd do**. Attribute claims to the sources actually read. Distinguish recurring themes from isolated anecdotes and explain plausible context for disagreements as an inference. Avoid broad labels based on a single review. Explain what changes in the plan, such as selecting a courtyard room, arriving earlier, or choosing another branch.
4. If a source is blocked, login-only, paywalled, unreadable, or times out, skip it and switch to an accessible equivalent. A search snippet is a lead to a readable page. Never imply you read a page you could not open, or require the traveler to change networks or create accounts. If the research tool can access a source the traveler may not, give a usable alternative or a local name/address for navigation and official alternatives for critical actions.
5. If only one independent source is readable or reports conflict, label the recommendation provisional where that matters. State the gap and its practical consequence. Verify opening, prices, reservation policies, accessibility and safety with the relevant operator or authority. Keep planning using verified facts and clearly identified experience signals.

## 中文流程

1. 先做候选短名单。对明显影响时间、费用或舒适度的推荐，核对同一分店和地址、酒店及相关房型、景区入口或票种、实际到访时间。用当地名称查「隔音」「楼梯」「排队」「踩雷」及当地语种表达，同时找好评和差评。
2. 证据可得时，每个关键推荐尽量对照两个以上独立平台或来源类型。优先看带实际日期、现场细节、照片或相似同行者背景的实测。比较同季节、同到访时段、同房型及相近预算。识别转载、重复和赞助内容，相同叙述只算一次。各平台星级的口径有差异，按体验维度比较。
3. 用「适合谁／反复被夸的点／反复被吐槽的点／分歧与适用条件／怎么选」综合输出。引用实际读到的页面，区分重复信号和个别反馈。用条件解释分歧时标明推断，例如周末排队、临街房噪音，或高峰期出菜慢，并把它转化为具体安排，例如选内院房、调整到访时段或换分店。
4. 遇到地域限制、登录墙、付费墙、超时或正文读不到，跳过该站并改查可访问的同类来源。搜索摘要只用来找线索。只引用实际读过的内容，流程无需用户切换网络或注册账号。若研究端能查到但用户可能打不开，给可用替代入口或当地名称与地址，关键操作附官方入口。
5. 只有单一独立来源或意见冲突时，标注影响判断的缺口、暂定结论和实际后果。开放、价格、预约、无障碍和安全等到官方或经营方核验。把已核验的事实和标明性质的体验线索继续整合进攻略。

## Example / 示例

Illustrative only. One traveler forum praises a hotel's station access, while booking-site reviews repeatedly mention traffic in street-facing rooms. A quiet-room review concerns a different room category. Recommend a courtyard-facing room for light sleepers, subject to confirmation by the hotel, and give a quieter alternative if that room is unavailable. If a map platform is inaccessible, skip it and use the hotel's address and station walking directions from a readable source.

虚构示例。论坛常夸一家酒店离车站近，预订平台多位住客提到临街房有车声，另一条「很安静」的评论对应不同房型。对浅眠者，推荐先向酒店确认能否订到内院房，并给订不到时的安静备选。若地图评价打不开，跳过该平台，附酒店地址和可访问来源里的步行路线。
