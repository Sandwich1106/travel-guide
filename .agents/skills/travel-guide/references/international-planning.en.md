# International travel field guide

[简体中文](international-planning.md) · [Cross-platform reviews and access fallbacks](community-evidence.md)

Cover the modules below for a full international guide. Mark inapplicable items, unreleased inventory, and prices that cannot be verified publicly. Give the effect, official channel, who can confirm, and recheck deadline. For early-stage planning, check itinerary-defining constraints first. For a revision, recheck the affected modules.

## 1. Visas, entry, and transit

- Establish passport issuer/type, residence or filing jurisdiction, purpose, length of stay, transit countries and any self-transfer, baggage collection, airport change, or overnight connection. Branch on missing facts. Verify destination and transit rules separately with immigration authorities, consulates and their designated application centers. Cross-check boarding requirements with the airline or IATA Travel Centre when relevant.
- State the applicable result and dates: exemption, electronic authorization, visa on arrival, eVisa, consular visa, or transit permit. List required documents, photo and translation rules, appointment and biometrics, submission and collection, official application link, first filing date and suggested latest filing date. Itemize government and service fees, applicable extras and total in the original currency. Separate appointment wait, processing and return delivery. Label calendar vs. working days and estimates vs. published times.
- Distinguish validity window, number of entries, maximum stay, and how days are counted. Check passport validity and blank pages, onward ticket, lodging, funds, health and customs declarations as applicable. Flag non-refundable commitments that depend on eligibility or approval and give cancellable options.

## 2. SIM, eSIM, roaming, and offline backup

- Compare local physical SIM, local/travel eSIM and home roaming for actual trip duration, crossings, rural legs, data use, tethering and local number/SMS needs. Check exact phone model, sales region and carrier lock. Recommend a primary plan and fallback, with purchase channel, airport or city shop, hours, ID registration, setup and activation, top-ups, validity, data limits, throttling, tethering and voice/SMS.
- Compare all-in price for the same period and identify the host network. Use operator coverage and recent local reports or independent tests relevant to the actual route. Describe the geographic and date limits of speed evidence. Where evidence is thin, rank by coverage, plan limitations and available feedback. Prepare offline maps, copyable addresses, pickup contacts and a no-signal rendezvous for remote legs.

## 3. Leg-by-leg transport and gaps in maps

- **Give a door-to-door time or range for every leg.** Include origin/hotel to airport, flight, immigration and bags, local transfers, walks, activity connections and return. For each leg give endpoints, mode/operator, departure window or service, in-vehicle time, waits/check-in/collection, total elapsed time, price and unit, payment or booking, latest departure, and fallback. Add time windows without double-counting buffers. For irregular services give a range and a confirmation channel.
- **Keep searching when maps show no service.** Search both directions with local stop names, airports, ports, corridor and town names. Use airport/port, tourism board, operator, lodging or transfer-provider timetables, PDFs, notices and public contact pages. Check recent trip reports for boarding details. Confirm weekday and holiday schedules, direction, flag stops, ticketing or cash, baggage, last useful departure and late-arrival fallback. When sources are old or conflict, flag the uncertainty and price a fallback. For Nadi Airport to Pacific Harbour, check tourism and coach-operator sources, then test the connection after landing.
- **Compare transit, taxi and self-drive for the actual legs.** Give transit boarding, alighting, transfers, fare card/app purchase, top-up, deposit/refund and tapping rules. Calculate an initial top-up from planned rides. For taxis or rideshare give typical fare range, basis, tolls and airport/night extras, and payment method. For driving include pickup/drop-off, traffic, parking, fuel/charging, tolls, excess, deposit and one-way fees. Compare time, comfort and cost. Verify both legal and rental-company rules for the license issuer, original license, translation or applicable IDP, age, card and vehicle limits. Include driving side, road conditions, child seats, winter gear and vehicle ferry restrictions as relevant.
- **Lock down unusual transfers first.** Check seller, actual operator, booking lead time, luggage and passenger rules, credentials, weather/daylight, refund, waits and fallback for each seaplane, regional flight, boat, 4WD or buggy leg. For Maldives resort changes, check any hub routing and whether both resorts must arrange transfers. For Vanuatu volcano visits, check sellable domestic legs, airport 4WD, guide, volcano status and return. In the Whitsundays, check the actual island, buggy license and deposit, rental hours, and included transport, walking and shuttles.
- **Charters and private transfers.** Find licensed providers, hotel concierge or authorized local agency channels and public contacts. Give a copyable inquiry with dates, endpoints, party, baggage, pickup, stops and return. Identify per-vehicle/vessel/aircraft or per-person pricing, crew, waiting, fuel, taxes, cancellations, weather and insurance. Mark concierge availability pending when unverified.

## 4. Payments, exchange, and cash

For relevant taxis, tickets and payments, use [price pitfalls](price-pitfalls.md#english) to show a comparable fare, what to confirm on the spot, and a concrete alternative.

- Check cash, physical cards and contactless wallets such as Apple Pay for actual lodgings, restaurants, transit, taxis, markets, tickets, deposits and tips. Account for town/rural differences, card network and foreign-card acceptance, minimums, change, small denominations and offline situations.
- Calculate cash as confirmed cash-only expenses plus estimated small purchases and a contingency, less local currency already held. Show per-trip range and assumptions, initial amount to withdraw/exchange, replenishment points, and usable ATM or exchange channels. Keep deposits or holds separate.
- Compare all-in home-currency cost on the same purchase for exchange, ATM, physical cards and wallets. Include issuer FX fees and rates, local ATM or merchant surcharges and dynamic currency conversion (DCC). Separate taxes from payment fees. Give a break-even or a pending item if rates are missing. Show known differences between local-currency settlement and DCC offers.

## 5. Choose the area before the hotel

- Score candidate areas by actual door-to-door commutes to key anchors, airports/stations, early departures and late returns. Compare last service, transfers, slopes, night access, food, quiet and budget. Give searchable boundaries and alternative bases for priorities such as early flights and quiet sleep. Quantify the time and cost penalty of a distant brand hotel.
- Cross-check independent guest reports for the exact property and relevant room category or orientation. Separate breakfast availability, inclusion, hours and actual taste. Verify Chinese-language service and staffing restrictions, in-room/shared/paid laundry and dryers, and pool season, hours, maintenance and child restrictions. Verify facilities and policies with operators. Use reviews for real-world experience.
- Compare hotel-direct and accessible sellers such as Trip.com/Ctrip and Booking.com for the same dates, room, occupancy, meals, refund and payment terms. Capture currency, taxes, resort fees, deposits, membership eligibility, availability, time and links. Label display-only quotes and missing checkout fees.

## 6. Airfare and when to book

- Compare direct airlines and accessible sellers such as Trip.com/Ctrip and Booking.com for equivalent airports, dates, passenger count, cabin, baggage and fare rules. Distinguish protected tickets from self-transfers. Include bags, seats, fees, exchange, airport changes and required overnight stays. Record who handles after-sales issues, all-in price and lookup time.
- Use comparable route/date/cabin history or credible price insights alongside live and flexible-date quotes. State evidence coverage and give a reasonable range, buy threshold, watch deadline and rationale. If no history is available, label the current comparable range and alternate dates accordingly. Weigh holiday demand, capacity and scarce downstream connections against waiting. Suggest tracking without setting it up unless authorized.

## 7. Put food quality first

- Select for flavor, signature dishes, ingredients, consistency and local character. Fit the traveler's palate, spice tolerance, diet and budget. Cross-check local-language and traveler reports for the exact branch. Give what to order, party fit, per-person price, queues, booking, closing breaks and nearby fallback. Separate food from ambiance and fame.
- Include Michelin picks when they match taste and budget. Verify the current Michelin Guide listing and branch and distinguish stars, Bib Gourmand and other selections. Check menus, drinks/service extras, booking windows, deposits, dress and cancellation. Provide strong local alternatives when the guide lacks coverage or the options do not suit. Have the venue verify allergies and cross-contact.

## 8. Activities, photography, conditions, and packing

- Give price range and unit, extras, booking requirement, deadline and channel, credentials, attire, age/weight/skill limits, included or rental equipment, weather cancellation and refunds for key activities. For high-risk activities check official opening, guide credentials, rescue and insurance coverage.
- Give the precise lawful photo spot and access. Include extra walking distance/time, climb or steps, queue, restrictions and crowded periods. For sunrise/sunset verify local date and time zone, solar bearing, terrain/building obstruction, access, visibility, and transport before/after. Give a fallback and latest departure. Keep astronomical times, weather forecast and actual sightline separate.
- Pack for the season, booked activities and carrier limits. Separate essential, rentable and optional gear. Cover footwear, layers, water, navigation, sun and insect protection, suitable first aid and offline documents. Check route-specific traction and winter equipment requirements. Separate mandatory vaccination, transit-triggered requirements and health recommendations using destination rules and authoritative travel medicine sources, with individual timing confirmed by a travel clinic. Add medication import restrictions, rescue cover, emergency contacts, adapters, laundry and baggage delivery when relevant.
