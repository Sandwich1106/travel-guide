# Research and itinerary audit

## Match evidence to the decision

| Check | Use first | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry, transit, safety | Destination and transit immigration agencies, consulates, traveler-nationality advisories | Passport type, residence or visa, connecting-ticket arrangements, baggage collection, airport changes, overnight stops, effective dates |
| Opening, last entry, reservations | Venue notices and booking pages | Specific date, weekday and holiday exceptions, ticket category, ID and age requirements |
| Schedules, fares, cancellations | Operator timetables, checkout pages and alerts | Direction, station, platform or entrance, actual walk and transfer, last service, release date for future inventory |
| Lodging and food | Dated room availability, hotel or restaurant policies | All-in room cost, occupancy, deposits, late check-in, meal hours, dietary needs, cancellation, access and stairs |
| Weather and outdoors | Weather agency, emergency notices, land or attraction manager | Use seasonal normals for distant dates and forecasts near departure. Check daylight, route closures, waves, tides, equipment and turnaround where relevant |
| Experience and crowding | [Independent community reports](community-evidence.md), local guides and crowd data | Recent visit date, season, time slot and traveler profile. Recheck operating facts with the operator |

Search destination-language sources where useful. Where official sources disagree, check effective dates, applicability and the issuing body. Explain unresolved conflicts and their implications. For map links, verify the exact address or entrance. Provide searchable local text if a deep link cannot be checked.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified** means the relevant primary source supports the date or conditions. Keep live availability separate from a confirmed booking.
- **Estimate** means a range with a stated basis, such as a mapping estimate, dated quote, historical spending, or climate averages.
- **Pending** means the date, availability, or traveler condition is unconfirmed. Name the check, responsible party, deadline, and impact.
- **Experience signal** means a firsthand account or pattern. Label its context and link the specific source.

For high-impact facts, record a direct link, verification date, applicable date, status, and time of lookup when price or stock changes quickly.

## Check before delivery

Use the [traveler interview](traveler-interview.md#english) to check primary and fallback plans, bookings and split logistics against each person’s allergies, dietary preferences and activity restrictions.

For a full international trip, check each module in the [field guide](international-planning.en.md). Test the most fragile connection using every leg's time and cutoff. Check that the cash recommendation reconciles with planned spending, flight and hotel comparisons match terms, and sunrise/sunset spots are accessible both ways.

- Every overnight has a place, dates reconcile across midnight, and arrival, storage, check-in/out, and departure fit.
- Critical legs fit door to door with queues, meals, rest and transfer buffers. Check outward and return services. Work backwards from hard deadlines and verify fallback transport.
- Opening and availability are distinct. Include booking windows and time zones, sales links, ID requirements and cancellation deadlines where relevant.
- Reconcile currencies, taxes, exchange rates, people, rooms and nights. Count paid amounts in total cost and show balances separately. Treat deposits as cash tied up. Avoid double-counting passes and covered fares.
- Check traveler-specific limits such as walking, slopes, steps, lifts, toilets, allergies, car seats and baggage. Mark critical access or severe-allergy claims pending until supported by the operator.
- Run relevant disruption scenarios such as missed trains, rain, closures or sold-out tickets. Check that the substitute is open and reachable and preserves the next commitment.
- Add relevant driving documents, parking, one-way fees, remote-area supplies, coverage and evacuation. Verify destination emergency contacts and travel advisories with official sources.
