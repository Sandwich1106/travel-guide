# Budget and value / 按预算设计旅行

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

### 先确定预算边界和想保留的体验

- 明确币种、人均还是全团、目标预算和硬上限，是否含往返、签证、保险、装备及备用金，哪些已付。若数字不完整，用有标识的情景区间继续研究。问最值得保留的一两件事、可让步的日期/天数/路线，以及睡眠、体力、舒适度或同行者底线。预算高低都以同样细度做攻略。
- 先找**能保住核心体验的最低可行总价**，并为实际预算设计推荐方案。低预算明确时优先做好这档，升级只列有意义的选项。预算有弹性或未定时，可比较省钱、均衡、舒适或高端方案，前提是体验确实有差异。高端也说明溢价换来的是时间、私密性、向导质量、住宿本身还是更稀缺的体验，哪些花费可以不加。
- 如原目的地超出硬上限，给最低可行成本、缺口和主要成本来源。依次尝试更换日期/出发门户、缩短天数、减少换城、单独购买核心体验、共摊、降低住宿成本或寻找同类近程目的地。保留用户定下的限制。每个替代说清保住了什么、实际损失了什么，以及省多少钱或多花多少时间。只有能核验的组合才标为可行；价格或库存待查时给条件、查证入口和下一步。

### 主动找到低曝光但可行的买法

- 用当地语言搜索公共交通/长途客运运营商、公营园区和营地、官方合乘、合规当地小团、非核心城区住宿、区域铁路、联票与点对点票、工作日/淡季或替代入口。对照官方渠道和近期实录，核验对外国旅客可售、会员或居民资格、夜间到达、步行与行李。低价候选也做门到门路线、订票步骤、返程和备选。
- 比较陆路、夜车、廉航、渡轮时，把换站、通关、错过接续、到站打车、行李附加费、额外过夜、可用游玩时间和休息算进去。夜车若确实替代一晚住宿，只扣一次。通票加订座费后再和单买比。跨境必须核对对应口岸、方向、国籍和运营方。
- 对少见或流传很广的省钱玩法，核对是否允许载客、运营与活动许可、保险及实际条件。园区的骑行、摩托、自驾、步行和向导规则按**具体公园与线路**核验。缺少许可或承运确认时列为待核验，提供合法可预订替代。用设备、疲劳、暴露时间和应急成本评估可行性。

### 同口径算清每一档

| 方案 | 保留的核心体验 | 人均与全团总价 | 额外耗时、休息与舒适度 | 减少或增加了什么 | 成立条件 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- 对齐日期、人数、天数与包含项，分开机票等固定成本、住宿/车辆等共享成本、人头费和选配。加税费、许可、行李、最后一公里、换汇手续费及备用金。可退押金列现金占用，已付列入总成本并与待付分列。写原币、汇率依据、查询日、真实可售/估算/待确认。给每档的预算余量或缺口，并用增量金额比较值得升级的单项。
- 区分「当前可核验的最低可行组合」与「可能更低的估算」。加价、取消或低价卖完会改变结论时，给购买阈值和切换条件。可用数字不足时以有依据的区间交付。

### 如何应用

- **Safari 可以拆着买。** 对同一目标比较城市或门户镇住宿＋园方/合乘观兽、允许的自驾＋公营营地、以及营地套餐、私人向导、野奢或飞入式接驳。逐项核算园区费、车辆、餐食、向导和机场接驳。Hell’s Gate 可以研究骑行，但要说明与其他园区的观兽目标差异。Kruger 的公营营地、自驾和园方观兽车是独立选项，实际许可和名额按日期查。[Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/hells-gate-national-park/) · [SANParks](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/kruger/useful-information/vital-information) · [园方观兽车](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/kruger/what-to-do/activities/game-drives)
- **陆路本身可以是体验。** 例如比较有售的毛里塔尼亚客车厢、公路客运和持证沙漠行程。SNIM 公告明确禁止搭乘载矿车厢，客运由 SOMASERT 管理，出发前核实当日是否有客运服务。欧洲跨境把区域车、大巴、夜车、快车与通票按实际路线和订座费比较；南部非洲跨境大巴同样检查证件、实际通关用时和抵达后的交通。[SNIM 公告](https://snim.com/en/snim-warns-against-unauthorized-and-dangerous-desert-excursions-ore-trains) · [Interrail 订座费](https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees) · [Intercape 跨境条款](https://www.intercape.co.za/terms-and-conditions/)

## English

### Start with the budget and the experience worth keeping

- Establish currency, per-person vs. party, target and hard cap, airfare, visas, insurance, gear, contingency and payments already made. If needed, research labeled budget scenarios. Ask what one or two experiences matter most, what dates or route can flex, and which sleep, exertion, comfort or group limits are firm. Apply the same planning detail at every budget.
- Find the **lowest viable all-in cost that preserves the core experience**, then optimize the recommendation for the stated budget. Prioritize a complete plan at the requested budget. When budget is flexible or unknown, compare lean, balanced and comfort/premium variants where they offer meaningful differences. Explain what premiums buy in time, privacy, guide quality, lodging or scarce experiences and which are optional.
- If the trip exceeds the cap, show the lowest viable cost, shortfall and largest cost drivers. Try dates or gateways, shorter stays, fewer moves, unbundling, sharing costs, cheaper bases or a nearer destination with the same core appeal. Preserve firm limits. Name the experience kept or lost, money saved and time added. Label options with unconfirmed price or inventory as conditional and give a way to verify them.

### Find overlooked low-cost options

- Search in local languages for public transport and coaches, public parks and camps, official shuttles, licensed shared groups, alternate bases, regional rail, passes and point-to-point tickets, cheaper dates and alternate entrances. Use operators and recent trip reports to check foreign visitor access, eligibility, late arrival, walks and baggage. Give the low-cost option complete door-to-door instructions, booking, return and fallback.
- Compare overland, overnight transport, budget air and ferries with station changes, borders, missed connections, last-mile taxis, baggage fees, extra nights, useful travel time and rest. Count a hotel night saved by a night train once. Compare pass plus reservations against individual tickets. Verify the exact border, direction, passport and operator for cross-border travel.
- For unusual options, verify passenger permission, operator/activity licensing, insurance and actual conditions. Check cycling, motorbikes, driving, walking and guide rules at the specific park and route. When permission or service is unclear, make it pending and offer a lawful bookable alternative. Include equipment, fatigue, exposure and contingency in feasibility.

### Compare on the same basis

| Option | Core experience kept | Per-person and party totals | Added time, rest and comfort | What is gained or lost | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Compare matching dates, party size, duration and inclusions. Separate fixed costs, shared rooms/vehicles, per-person fees and options. Add taxes, permits, baggage, last-mile, FX fees and contingency. Separate refundable holds and paid vs. outstanding. Record original currency, conversion basis, lookup date and live/estimated/pending status. Show headroom or shortfall and compare incremental upgrade costs.
- Distinguish the lowest verified viable combination from a lower hypothetical estimate. Give a buy threshold and switching trigger where sellouts or price changes alter the choice. Use sourced ranges where exact figures are unavailable.

### Examples

- **Unbundle safari.** Compare a city or gateway base plus park/shared drives, permitted self-drive plus public camping, and lodge packages, private guides or fly-in stays. Itemize park fees, vehicle, meals, guide and transfers. Cycling in Hell’s Gate may suit a different wildlife objective from other parks. Check public camps, self-drive and park-run game drives in Kruger for the dates. [Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/hells-gate-national-park/) · [SANParks](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/kruger/useful-information/vital-information) · [Park game drives](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/kruger/what-to-do/activities/game-drives)
- **Make the overland leg part of the experience.** Compare authorized passenger rail, road transport and licensed desert trips in Mauritania. SNIM prohibits riding ore wagons and identifies SOMASERT as the passenger service manager. Confirm service for the date. In Europe compare regional rail, coach, sleeper, express and passes with reservation supplements. For southern African cross-border coaches, check documents, actual border delays and onward transport.
