# Travel guide deliverables

## Planning and booking edition

1. **Decision brief.** Recommended route, whom it suits, dates, cities and nights, cost range, key experiences, urgent bookings and unresolved dependencies. Compare options on the dimensions the traveler values.
2. **Daily overview and route.** Give dates, lodging bases, neighborhoods, anchors, travel and walking time, and costs. For key stops explain why, best time, duration, entrance, booking and a fallback. Include nearby meals and optional stops. For key recommendations summarize [cross-platform review findings](community-evidence.md).
3. **Lodging and transport.** Compare bases using actual transfers, walking, price and late return. Identify station or airport terminals, operators, outward/return options and ticket channels. Evaluate passes against the actual itinerary and exclusions.
4. **Booking and recheck list.** Sort by deadline or prerequisite. Include status, dates, local release time, direct channel, latest quote, refund terms and fallback. Flag entry permits and other dependencies before high-cost commitments.
5. **Budget.** Give unit price in the original currency, quantities, shared vs. individual costs, totals, tax and exchange assumptions, amounts paid and outstanding, and a range. Separate optional spending and contingency.
6. **Before departure and in emergencies.** Include relevant documents, connectivity, payment, power, baggage, etiquette, holidays, insurance, verified contacts and offline addresses. Assign rechecks to booking, predeparture or same-day stages.

## Mobile card

Keep each day scannable. Include where to start, the latest departure, booked times, easy-to-miss entrances, meal and rest options, return route and fallback triggers. Use verified navigation links plus copyable local addresses. Match the times and booking status in the full plan.

## Audit and replanning

Rank issues by failure risk, material impact, then optional improvements. Link evidence, name affected dates, and offer the smallest workable fix. When replanning, give the immediate action, decision deadline, affected bookings, incremental cost, updated route and return. Explain any unavoidable trade-off. Use Markdown unless another format is requested.
