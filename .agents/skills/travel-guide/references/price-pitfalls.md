# Overcharging and price checks / 防宰客与价格核验

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

对行程中容易遇到临时报价、隐形收费或冒充官方渠道的节点做简短提醒。按具体路线、商家和场景说明，避免把整个国家或所有司机一概而论。优先覆盖机场、码头、夜间抵达、景点门口和替代交通少的地方。

1. **先建比价基准。** 出租车按同一起点、终点、方向、时段、车型和人数比。分开列主管部门计价规则、可核验的整车一口价、近期旅客实付或口头报价。注明币种、按人还是按车、数据日期、夜间/机场/预约/行李/过路费及等待费。价格超参考区间时，先核对是否同一种服务及有无明示加价。历史实付和旅客经验作为线索，关键报价尽量用官方费率或两个独立可用渠道交叉核对。
2. **告诉用户当场怎么做。** 上车前确认打表或约定的含费总价，核对币种及收费单位。对网约车核车牌和司机，核对是否要求改用现金或取消平台订单。备用写到具体上车点、班次、时间、价格、支付和能否赶上下一程。可附一句当地语言或英语询价，例如 “Is that the total fare for both of us, including any fees?”
3. **同样核对门票、向导、购物和餐饮。** 区分官方票与代理服务费，说明赠送项目、小费、骑乘/拍照、绕路购物、菜单计价和动态货币转换等什么时候需要提前问。只写本路线有证据或相关的情形。
4. **给处理方式。** 价格没谈清可先离开并换已核验的渠道。发生争议时优先离开紧张场景，有条件就保留订单、车牌、收据和报价，在安全处向平台、酒店、监管机构或旅游部门反馈。联系方式按目的地查实。把最可能遇到的场景和备选同步到随身卡，预算用可执行的价格而非最乐观的个例。

### 例子

- **斐济 Port Denarau → Nadi Town。** 有旅客经验称约 FJ$15，码头报价有 FJ$30–35 的反馈。一家接送商的公开价目表曾列 FJ$21.85/车（1–4 人）。分别标为经验、现场报价与商家价，按实际日期重查，区分码头、岛上酒店和机场。斐济旅游局也区分打表与固定计价车辆。备用可查到 Nadi Town 的 Westbus，确认站点、末班和 eTransport 卡；Bula Bus 用于岛内，不能当作进城班次。[斐济旅游局出租车指南](https://www.fiji.travel/things-to-know/getting-around/land-airport-transfers) · [接送商价目表](https://denaraudestinations.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Destinations-Price-List-25.pdf) · [码头旅客实录](https://www.kellylaughsalot.com/2026/09/05/port-denarau-fiji/) · [Denarau 本地交通](https://buladenarau.com/bula-your-arrival/)
- **埃及开罗、吉萨。** 分开查机场出租车、网约车、景点门票和额外体验。上车前看计价器或应用里的费用，核对车牌，遇到临时报高价时可改走官方候车点或有记录的预约渠道。用埃及文物部门核对官方票，保留票据。需要反馈时复核当地旅游部门的联系方式。[澳洲官方埃及出行提示](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/egypt) · [埃及文物部门购票说明](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/news/the-giza-pyramids-plateau-started-operating-four-self-service-machines-to-purchase-site-tickets-using-bank-cards/) · [埃及文物部门联系方式](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/contact-us/)

## English

Flag points where ad hoc quotes, undisclosed extras or unofficial sellers can affect this itinerary. Focus on the specific route, seller and situation, especially airports, ports, late arrivals and places with few alternatives.

1. **Build a comparable baseline.** Match endpoints, direction, time, vehicle and party size. Separate regulated meter rules, verifiable fixed quotes, recent paid fares and verbal quotes. Give currency, per-person or per-car basis, date, and night, airport, booking, baggage, toll and waiting charges. If a quote is high, first check whether it covers a different service or disclosed add-on. Use historical reports as leads and cross-check consequential prices with an official tariff or two independent usable sources where available.
2. **Say what to do at pickup.** Confirm the meter or all-in fixed amount, unit and currency before boarding. Match ride-hail plate and driver and check for requests to cancel or pay off-app. Name the fallback stop, service, elapsed time, price and payment method, and whether it protects the next connection. Add a short local-language question when useful.
3. **Check tickets, guides, retail and dining the same way.** Separate official admission from agent fees. Where relevant, flag charged extras, tipping, photos or rides, shopping detours, menu units and currency conversion. Include itinerary-specific evidence.
4. **Give a practical response.** If terms are unclear, use the checked alternative. In a dispute prioritize getting to a safe place, retain receipts, plate and price records when practical, then use the verified platform, hotel, regulator or tourism contact. Put the most relevant checks and fallback in the mobile card. Budget for an attainable fare.

Distinguish marina, hotel and airport pickups. Verify metered vs. fixed fare and check Westbus, last departure and eTransport as a fallback. The Bula Bus is the island loop.
