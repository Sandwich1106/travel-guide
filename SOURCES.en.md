# Research sources

Updated 18 November 2026. Author itineraries, trip reports and experiential guides must have a verified publication or substantive update date on or after 1 January 2022. Crawl dates, copyright years and travel years in titles are not publication dates. Undated sources are excluded from the eligible guide set until checked.

See the [dated Chinese source table](SOURCES.md) and [full classified register](docs/research/source-catalogue.md). The 150 discovered URLs are not 150 eligible guides: five currently have recorded post-2021 dates. Current official rules, operator terms, climate data and software references are classified separately and checked for validity rather than represented as dated travel reports.
